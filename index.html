        📖How to Read NexariOS
        ☄️ HR Signals
        The HR Signals tab ranks every batter playing today by their likelihood of hitting a home run, using the Overmind Composite Metric (OCM).
        Strong 18%+ HR probability. Highest confidence. Prioritize these for single-game and parlay legs.
        Medium 11–17% HR probability. Solid situational play — good for longer odds parlays.
        Light Under 11% probability. Low confidence. Use only when odds offer significant value.
        OCM Score 0–100 composite score combining batter power stats (HR rate, SLG, OPS) against pitcher vulnerability (ERA, HR allowed). Higher = stronger matchup.
        Odds Pill Live line from your selected sportsbook. Switch books using the Book selector above the list. N/A means no line is posted yet.
        ⚾️ Games
        Shows every game on today's slate. Tap any row to expand the full roster for both teams, including the probable starting pitcher (SP). Pre-game rows show the scheduled ET start time. Live rows show the current score and inning. Final rows show the final score.
        📊 Accuracy
        Tracks how well the engine's Strong-tier picks have converted into actual home runs over time. System Tracker shows lifetime pick history. HR & RBI Tracker shows rolling 7-day and 30-day windows. Season Leaders shows live 2026 MLB leaders in HR, Hits, and RBI so you can cross-reference top producers against the engine's current signals.
        Note Historical pick tracking requires a KV backend (in progress). Current pick counts will show 0 until that goes live.
        🔍 Search
        Search any player name or team name. Results show their OCM score, tier, opponent pitcher, and position for the current day's games.
