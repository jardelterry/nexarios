        🧬 System Intelligence
        OCM (Overmind Composite Metric)
        Blends matchup quality, power profile, recent form, and volatility into a single score. Higher OCM means stronger overall HR environment for that player.

        HR Base
        Baseline HR expectation for the matchup. OCM can be high even when HR base is modest if other factors align (bullpen exposure, park, streaks).

        Playable
        A signal is “playable” when OCM, odds, volatility, and role all clear internal thresholds. Some high‑OCM players are filtered out if risk or price is off‑profile.

        Hits & Misses
        A “hit” is a projected player who homers and was marked playable. Scratches, benchings, and wrong‑lineup cases are tracked separately so they don’t distort accuracy.

        Unlisted HRs
        Players who homer without being in signals. These feed back into future calibration and help refine edges and blind spots.

        Streaks
        Hot, cold, and team streaks describe recent HR clustering. They don’t override the model but tilt edges when everything else is close.

        System Performance
        Each system is tracked by record, units, ROI, and volatility. The goal is not just raw hits, but stable, repeatable edges over time.

        Daily Accuracy Blocks
        Each day is summarized by hits, misses, accuracy %, units, and best/worst pick. This is your high‑level read on how NexariOS behaved in that environment.
