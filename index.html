        📖How to Read NexariOS
        ☄️ HR Signals
        Round-robin across all games — best pick from each game appears before any game's second pick. Each card shows the player's season stats, tier, OCM score, and live DraftKings odds. ☄️ before a name = hot streak (hit/HR/RBI in 3+ straight games). ❄️ = cold streak (0 hits/HR/RBI in 3+ straight).
        Strong18%+ HR probability — highest confidence. Best for single-game props and parlay legs.
        Medium11–17% probability — solid situational play, good for longer-odds parlays.
        LightUnder 11% — value plays only when odds offer significant edge.
        OCMOvermind Composite Metric 0–100. Combines batter power vs pitcher vulnerability. Higher = stronger HR edge in this matchup.
        🏠 HR vs SPCareer home runs against this specific starting pitcher. Only shown when 2 or more HR in past matchups.
        📊 Stat Glossary
        These stats appear under each player's name and in the advanced stats row.
        AVGBatting Average — hits divided by at-bats. .300+ is elite. League average is around .250.
        SLGSlugging Percentage — total bases divided by at-bats. Measures power. .500+ is excellent. Power hitters often sit .450–.600.
        OPSOn-base Plus Slugging — OBP + SLG combined. Best single measure of offensive value. .800+ is above average. .900+ is elite.
        ISOIsolated Power — SLG minus AVG. Pure extra-base power metric. .200+ signals elite HR power. .150+ is solid.
        BarrelBarrel Rate — % of batted balls hit at optimal launch angle + exit velocity for max damage. 10%+ is excellent. Directly predicts HR output.
        HardHitHard Hit Rate — % of balls hit 95+ mph exit velocity. 40%+ is above average. High hard-hit + high launch angle = HR.
        EVAverage Exit Velocity in mph. 90+ mph avg EV signals consistent hard contact. 92+ is elite. Higher EV batter vs weak pitcher = strong HR setup.
